Question 73 marks

Phil wants to predict how much money he will have in his savings account each month. He uses the following recurrence relation to model his account balance from month to month.

V₀ = 8000, Vₙ₊₁ = 1.0025 × Vₙ + 230

Edrolo U3&4 6F Q4
aCalculate the annual interest rate for this annuity investment. (1 mark)
0.0025 × 100 × 12 = 3% per annum
bHow much would Phil have in his savings account after two months? (1 mark)
V₁ = 8250, V₂ = 1.0025 × 8250 + 230 = $8500.63
cHow many months will it take for Phil’s account balance to first reach $9000? (1 mark)
V₃ = 8751.88, V₄ = 9003.76 → 4 months
