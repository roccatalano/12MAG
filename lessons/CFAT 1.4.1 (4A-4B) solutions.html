(mosquito bites)² = 282 − 49 × mosquito repellent sprays

Edrolo U3&4 3E Q7
aUsing the equation, estimate the number of mosquito repellent sprays a student would have applied if they received 6 mosquito bites. Round your answer to the nearest whole number. (2 marks)
6² = 282 − 49 × sprays → sprays = (282 − 36) ÷ 49 = 5.02…
5 sprays
bIs the estimate in part a reliable? Justify your answer. (1 mark)
Yes. The estimate is reliable because it is an interpolation.
