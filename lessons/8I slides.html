Key ideas

Key definitions and formulas

Key definitions

Perpetuityan annuity that pays out exactly the interest earned each period, so it lasts forever
Principalthe amount invested, V₀: it never changes
Growth multiplierR = 1 + r ÷ (100 × p)
Linkperpetuity is to annuity as interest-only loan is to reducing balance loan

Key formulas

RecurrenceV₀ = principal, Vₙ₊₁ = RVₙ − Dthe value stays at V₀
PaymentD = r ÷ (100 × p) × V₀r = annual rate, p = periods per year
Finance solverN = 1, PV = −V₀, FV = +V₀solve for PMT or I(%)
RearrangedV₀ = D ÷ (r ÷ (100p)) · r = 100p × D ÷ V₀for the principal or the rate

An annuity that never runs out

Calculating the payment from a perpetuity

Elizabeth invests her superannuation payout of $500 000 into a perpetuity that will provide a monthly income.
If the interest rate for the perpetuity is 6% per annum, what monthly payment will Elizabeth receive?
  1. 1FormulaD = r ÷ (100 × p) × V₀
  2. 2SubstituteD = 6 ÷ (100 × 12) × 500 000
  3. 3EvaluateD = 2500
  1. ✓AnswerElizabeth will receive $2500 every month from her investment.

Calculating the investment required to establish a perpetuity

Calculate how much money will need to be invested in a perpetuity account, earning interest of 4.8% per annum compounding monthly, if $300 will be withdrawn every month.
  1. 1FormulaD = r ÷ (100 × p) × V₀
  2. 2Substitute300 = 4.8 ÷ (100 × 12) × V₀ = 0.004 × V₀
  3. 3SolveV₀ = 300 ÷ 0.004 = 75 000
  1. ✓Answer$75 000 will need to be invested to establish the perpetuity investment.

WHAT · solve for r

Calculating the interest rate of a perpetuity

A university mathematics faculty has $30 000 to invest. It intends to award an annual mathematics prize of $1500 with the interest earned from investing this money in a perpetuity.
What is the minimum interest rate that will allow this prize to be awarded indefinitely?
  1. 1FormulaD = r ÷ (100 × p) × V₀, p = 1
  2. 2Substitute1500 = r ÷ (100 × 1) × 30 000 = r × 300
  3. 3Solver = 1500 ÷ 300 = 5
  4. 4Check on CASN = 1 · I = ? · PV = −30 000 · PMT = 1500 · FV = 30 000 · PpY = CpY = 1 → 5
  1. ✓AnswerThe minimum annual interest rate to award this prize indefinitely is 5%.

Calculating the interest rate of a perpetuity

A sports club has $40 000 to invest. It intends to award an annual prize of $1800 with the interest earned from investing this money in a perpetuity. What is the minimum interest rate that will allow this prize to be awarded indefinitely?
  1. 1FormulaD = r ÷ (100 × p) × V₀, p = 1
  2. 2Substitute1800 = r ÷ 100 × 40 000 = r × 400
  3. 3Solver = 1800 ÷ 400 = 4.5
