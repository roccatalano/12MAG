Question 176 marks

A family in Ballarat bought a new car. The table shows the value of the car, in dollars, at each age of the car, in years.

Age (years)Value ($)
036 000.00
130 600.00
226 010.00
322 108.50
418 792.23

aShow that the car is depreciating by reducing balance depreciation, and state the annual rate of depreciation. (1 mark)
Each value is 0.85 times the previous value (a constant ratio), so the rate is 15% per year.
bWrite a recurrence relation for Vn, the value of the car in dollars after n years. (1 mark)
V0 = 36 000, Vn+1 = 0.85Vn
cA financial planner fits a least squares regression line to the data in the table, with age as the explanatory variable. Use technology to find the equation of this line, with its coefficients rounded to the nearest whole number. (1 mark)
value = 35 284 − 4291 × age

Regression line: −$7626. Recurrence relation: $7087.
eWhich of the two predictions is more reasonable? Give a reason based on the data and the predictions. (2 marks)
The recurrence relation prediction of $7087. The regression line gives a negative value, which is impossible, and the data fall by a constant percentage, not a constant amount, so a linear model does not suit them.
