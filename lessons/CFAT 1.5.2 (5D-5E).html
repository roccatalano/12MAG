Question 72 marks

The quarterly sales ($000’s) of a canine clothing shop are displayed in the following table.

quarter 1quarter 2quarter 3quarter 4
sales ($000’s)33.561.352.043.4
Edrolo U3&4 4D Q2
aCalculate the seasonal index for quarter 1, correct to two decimal places. (1 mark)
mean = (33.5 + 61.3 + 52.0 + 43.4) ÷ 4 = 47.55
SI = 33.5 ÷ 47.55 = 0.70
bWhat does the rounded seasonal index for quarter 1 mean in terms of the amount of sales in quarter 1 compared to the average quarter? (1 mark)
On average, sales in quarter 1 are 70% of, or 30% less than, the average quarter.

Question 93 marks

Leonard conducted research into the number of alien sightings from 1975 to 2015 in the northern and southern hemispheres. He then used this data to construct regression equations to predict the number of alien sightings in each hemisphere for any given year.

Note: Leonard has used year = 0 to represent 1975.

The least squares regression equations are:

Southern hemisphere: alien sightings = 208 + 23.4 × year

Northern hemisphere: alien sightings = 361 + 41.2 × year

Edrolo U3&4 4E Q4
aEstimate the difference between the number of alien sightings in each hemisphere in the year 2030, correct to the nearest whole number. (1 mark)
year = 55: N = 361 + 41.2 × 55 = 2627, S = 208 + 23.4 × 55 = 1495
difference = 1132 alien sightings
bExplain why this prediction may be of limited reliability, even if Leonard’s original data was correct. (1 mark)
The prediction is an example of extrapolation since it is outside the range of recorded data.
cInterpret the slope of the regression line in terms of the change in the number of alien sightings in the northern hemisphere each year. (1 mark)
On average, the number of alien sightings in the northern hemisphere increases by 41.2 each year.
