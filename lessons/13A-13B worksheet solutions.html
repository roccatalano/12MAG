13A–13B

Graphs, networks and adjacency matrices · SOLUTIONS

Learning intentions
  • To be able to define and identify a graph, vertex, edge and loop.
  • To be able to find the degree of a vertex and the sum of degrees.
  • To be able to describe the features of a graph.
  • To be able to define and identify a planar graph and its faces.
  • To be able to apply Euler’s formula and use it to verify if a graph is planar.
  • To be able to use an adjacency matrix to represent a graph.

a
Show that the graph is a connected planar graph.

Example 4 · Using Euler’s formula
You try 4

A connected planar graph has six vertices and nine edges. How many faces does the graph have? Draw a connected planar graph with six vertices and nine edges.

v = 6   e = 9

v + f = e + 2 → 6 + f = 9 + 2 → f = 5

b
If a connected planar graph has five vertices and five faces, how many edges does the graph have?
5 − e + 5 = 2 → e = 8 edges
