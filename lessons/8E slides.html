Key definitions and formulas

Key definitions

Nthe total number of payments
I(%)the annual interest rate
PV / FVthe present (start) value / future (end) value
PMTthe amount of each regular payment
PpY = CpYpayments per year = compounding periods per year

Key formulas

Sign rulemoney you receive: +money you pay out: −
LoanPV +, PMT −FV − = still owing
AnnuityPV −, PMT +FV + = still in the account
Investment with additionsPV −, PMT −FV + = what you will get

Compound interest by rule

I can use the rule for determining the value of an investment with compounding interest or an asset with reducing-balance depreciation after a certain length of time.

e.g. Adrian invests $250 000 in an account earning compound interest at the rate of 6% per annum. Use a rule to find the value of the investment after 15 years.
  1. 1RuleVn = Rn × V0,   R = 1 + 6/100 = 1.06
  2. 2SubstituteV15 = 1.0615 × 250 000
  3. 3Evaluate= 599 139.548…
  1. ✓AnswerAfter 15 years the investment is worth $599 139.55.

The finance solver and its signs

Follow the money

  • money you receive (or are owed) is positive; money you pay out (or owe) is negative
  • enter six of N, I(%), PV, PMT, FV, PpY = CpY; solve for the one left
  • N counts payments (months, quarters…), not years
Sign rulereceive +, pay out −
PVPMTFV
Loan+−− still owing · 0 repaid
Annuity−++ still left · 0 paid out
Investment + additions−−+ you get it
Read the sign of FV: it tells you who owes whom.

Entering a loan in the finance solver

Isabelle takes out an interest-free loan of $30 000. She makes repayments of $1000 per week to pay off the loan. Isabelle wants to know how much she owes the bank 10 weeks after establishing the loan.

InputValue
N10
I(%)0
PV30 000
PMT−1000
FV(solve)
PpY52
CpY52

Loan: PV +, Pmt −. Investment/annuity: PV −, Pmt + (annuity) or − (additions). Interest-only loan or perpetuity: FV = −PV.

TI-Nspire

  1. Calculator page: Menu › Finance › Finance Solver.
  2. Enter N (number of payments), I(%) (annual rate), PV, Pmt, FV, PpY and CpY (payments/compounding per year; tab down). Money paid out by the person is negative, money received is positive.
  3. Leave the unknown, move the cursor to it and press enter to solve. Read it off and round as asked (round N up to whole payments).

ClassPad

  1. Main menu › Financial › Compound Interest.
  2. Enter N, I%, PV, PMT, FV, P/Y and C/Y (End of period payments). Money paid out by the person is negative, money received is positive.
  3. Tap the button of the unknown (N, I%, PV, PMT or FV) to solve it. Read it off and round as asked (round N up to whole payments).
  1. ✓Read offFV = −20 000: Isabelle still owes $20 000

  1. 1SignsPV and PMT are paid to the bank: negative

  3. 3SolveFV = 692 292.297…: positive, the bank gives it to Lars
  1. ✓AnswerAfter 5 years, Lars’ investment will be worth $692 292.30.

Balance and final payment of a reducing balance loan

Andrew borrows $20 000 at an interest rate of 7.25% per annum, compounding monthly. This loan will be repaid over 4 years with regular payments of $481.25 each month for 47 months followed by a final payment to fully repay the loan.
  1. aHow much does Andrew owe after 3 years? Round your answer to the nearest cent.
  2. bWhat is the final payment amount that Andrew must make to fully repay the loan within 4 years (48 months)? Round your answer to the nearest cent.
  1. 1a · EnterN = 36 · I = 7.25 · PV = 20 000 · PMT = −481.25 · FV = ? · PpY = CpY = 12
  2. 2a · SolveFV = −5554.3626…: negative, Andrew still owes
  3. 3b · EnterN = 48 · I = 7.25 · PV = 20 000 · PMT = −481.25 · FV = ? · PpY = CpY = 12
  4. 4b · SolveFV = +0.1079… ≈ 11 cents: overpaid, so the last payment is 11 cents less
  1. ✓Answers
    aAndrew owes $5554.36.
    bFinal payment = 481.25 − 0.11 = $481.14.

Balance and final payment of an annuity

Charlie invests $300 000 into an annuity, paying 5% interest per annum, compounding monthly. Over the next ten years, Charlie receives a payment of $3182 per month from the annuity for each month except the final month.
  1. aFind the value of the annuity after five years. Round your answer to the nearest cent.
  2. bFind the final payment from the annuity. Round your answer to the nearest cent.

  2. 2a · SolveFV = 168 612.24795…: positive, still owed to Charlie
  3. 3b · EnterN = 120 · I = 5 · PV = −300 000 · PMT = 3182 · FV = ? · PpY = CpY = 12

Show what you know

For renovations to her coffee shop, Sienne took out a reducing balance loan of $570 000, with interest calculated fortnightly.
The balance of the loan, in dollars, after n fortnights, Sn, can be modelled by the recurrence relation

    S0 = 570 000,   Sn+1 = 1.001 × Sn − 1193

Show that the compound interest rate for this loan is 2.6% per annum. (1 mark)
  1. 1Rate per fortnight1.001 − 1 = 0.001 = 0.1%
  2. 2Per year26 fortnights: (1.001 − 1) × 26 × 100 = 2.6
  1. ✓Answer(1.001 − 1) × 26 × 100 = 2.6% per annum. Start from 1.001, not from 2.6%.
