Question 192 marks

A study finds that the number of swimming pools in a suburb is strongly positively correlated with the rate of skin cancer in that suburb. A health reporter claims that swimming pools cause skin cancer. Explain why this conclusion may not be valid, and suggest a possible confounding variable.

Correlation does not imply causation. The association may be explained by a confounding variable such as the amount of sunshine in the suburb — sunnier suburbs tend to have more pools and also higher rates of sun exposure, which increases skin cancer risk.
