Key definitions and formulas

Key definitions

Time series datavalues of a variable recorded at regular time intervals; time is the EV (horizontal axis)
Trenda long-term increase or decrease
Cyclesup-and-down movements over periods longer than a year
Seasonalitya pattern that repeats each calendar period (year, month, week)
Structural changea sudden change in the established pattern
Outliera single value that stands out from the rest
Irregular fluctuationsrandom, unpredictable short-term changes: always present

Key formulas

Describing a plottrend · cycles · seasonalitythen structural change · outliers · irregular fluctuations
Trend linea line that ignores the fluctuationsslopes up: increasing · down: decreasing
Cycles vs seasonalitylonger than a year → cyclesone year or less, calendar-related → seasonality

APK · Year 11 · VCAA 2019 Exam 1 Q9

Rounding to significant figures

A least squares line is used to model the relationship between the monthly average temperature and latitude recorded at seven different weather stations. The equation of the least squares line is found to be
average temperature = 42.9842 – 0.877447 × latitude
When the numbers in this equation are correctly rounded to three significant figures, the equation will be
A.average temperature = 42.984 – 0.877 × latitude
B.average temperature = 42.984 – 0.878 × latitude
C.average temperature = 43.0 – 0.878 × latitude
D.average temperature = 42.9 – 0.878 × latitude
E.average temperature = 43.0 – 0.877 × latitude
  1. 1Intercept42.9842: first three significant figures 4, 2, 9; next digit 8 → round up: 43.0
  2. 2Slope0.877447: 8, 7, 7; next digit 4 → stays: 0.877
  1. ✓Answer · Eaverage temperature = 43.0 – 0.877 × latitude: E.
  2. What’s the distractor?
    A 14% rounded to three decimal places (42.984), not three significant figures.

Time series data and time series plots

Time series plots

  • time series data: one variable recorded at regular time intervals
  • time is always the EV: it goes on the horizontal axis
  • plot the points as for a scatterplot, then join consecutive points with straight lines
  • months, quarters or days can be coded 1, 2, 3 …
Time series datavalues recorded at regular time intervals
Never join the points in order of size: join them in time order.
Cambridge General Mathematics Units 3&4, 5A pp. 252–255 (data: CAS 1, birth rate 2011–2020)
Learn · 5A

What to look for in a time series plot

Six features

  • Trend: long-term up or down (draw a trend line through the fluctuations)
  • Cycles: peaks and troughs over periods longer than a year
  • Seasonality: peaks and troughs at the same time each calendar period
  • Structural change: a sudden change in the pattern · Outliers: single values that stand out
  • Irregular fluctuations: random changes, always there
Trendlong-term increase or decrease
Seasonalitycalendar-related repeating pattern

Seasonality needs a calendar period (year, month, week). Peaks that come every few years are cycles.
