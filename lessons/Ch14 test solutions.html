Question 146 marks

Water is pumped from a reservoir, S, to a town, T, through the network of pipes shown. The capacity of each pipe is in kilolitres per hour.

aDetermine the capacity of the cut that separates S and A from B, C and T. (1 mark)
24
bDetermine the maximum flow from the reservoir to the town, and identify the minimum cut. (2 marks)
22 kilolitres per hour; the minimum cut passes through pipes B → T and C → T.
cThe council increases the capacity of pipe C → T by 5 kilolitres per hour. Determine the new maximum flow, and explain why the maximum flow does not increase by 5. (1 mark)
23 kilolitres per hour. The cut through A → C, B → C and B → T, with capacity 23, now becomes the minimum cut.
dAn inspector wants to walk along every pipe exactly once, ignoring the direction of flow. Explain why the inspector cannot finish where they started, and state the two vertices where the walk must start and finish. (2 marks)
Exactly two vertices, A and C, have odd degree (3), so an Eulerian trail exists but not an Eulerian circuit. The walk must start at A and finish at C, or the reverse.
