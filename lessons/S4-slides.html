Learning intentions

  • To be able to construct the boxplot for displaying the distribution of a numerical data.
  • To be able to define and identify outliers.
  • To be able to construct both simple boxplots and boxplots with outliers.

Key ideas

Key definitions and formulas

Key definitions

Five-number summaryminimum, Q1, median, Q3, maximum
Boxplota picture of the five-number summary: the box holds the middle 50% of the data
Outliera value below the lower fence or above the upper fence, drawn as a dot
Whiskersrun from the box to the smallest and largest values that are not outliers

Key formulas

Median positionposition = n + 12
Median, n evenM = first middle + second middle2
Interquartile rangeIQR = Q3 − Q1
Rangerange = max − min
Lower fenceLF = Q1 − 1.5 × IQRx < LF → x is an outlier
Upper fenceUF = Q3 + 1.5 × IQRx > UF → x is an outlier

The five-number summary and the boxplot

Boxplot

A boxplot is a picture of the five-number summary. The box runs from Q1 to Q3 with a line at the median; the whiskers run out to the smallest and largest values.

Five-number summaryminimum, Q1, M, Q3, maximum
Each section holds about 25% of the data, however long it looks. A long whisker means the values are spread out, not that there are more of them.

Outliers: the 1.5 × IQR fences

Outlier

A value more than 1.5 IQRs below Q1 or above Q3. It is drawn as a dot, and the whisker stops at the last value inside the fence.

Lower fenceLF = Q1 − 1.5 × IQRx < LF → x is an outlier
Upper fenceUF = Q3 + 1.5 × IQRx > UF → x is an outlier
3 < 5 = LF → 3 is an outlier. Nothing is above 53, so the upper whisker runs to the largest value, 48; the lower whisker stops at 21.

        Reading shape from a boxplot

        Shape

        Look at where the median sits in the box and which whisker is longer: the long side is the tail.

        Rangerange = max − min

        Symmetric

        Median near the middle; whiskers about equal.

        Positively skewed

        Median to the left; long right whisker (tail to the right).

        Negatively skewed

        Median to the right; long left whisker (tail to the left).

        With outliers

        Dots separated from the box and whiskers by a gap.

        Describe with SOCS, like a sock: Shape · Outliers · Centre (the median) · Spread (IQR and range).
