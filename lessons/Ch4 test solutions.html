Question 46 marks

After a storm in Gippsland, teams of volunteers cleared fallen branches from sections of walking track of equal length. The number of volunteers in each team and the time taken, in hours, are shown below.

Volunteers1234568
Time (hours)24.512.88.66.75.34.63.4
A reciprocal transformation is applied to the number of volunteers, so that time is modelled against 1volunteers.

aUse technology to find the equation of the least-squares regression line for time against 1volunteers. Write the values rounded to two decimal places. (2 marks)
time = 0.57 + 24.03 × 1volunteers
bWrite the value of r² for the transformed data, rounded to four decimal places, and comment on how well the model fits. (1 mark)
r² = 0.9997. The model fits the transformed data very well.
cUse the rounded equation from part (a) to predict the time taken by a team of 7 volunteers, rounded to two decimal places. (1 mark)
time = 0.57 + 24.03 × 17 = 4.00 hours
dUse the model to find the minimum number of volunteers needed to clear a section in 2 hours or less, and comment on the reliability of this prediction. (2 marks)
2 = 0.57 + 24.03 × 1volunteers → volunteers = 24.03 ÷ 1.43 ≈ 16.8, so 17 volunteers. The prediction is unreliable because it is an extrapolation beyond the data (1 to 8 volunteers).
