Question 95 marks

Amanda competed in a 400 m swimming race. Her time for each lap of the 50-metre pool was recorded and the results are shown in the table.

lap12345678
time (seconds)3140445053555860
Edrolo U3&4 3E Q4
aApply a log transformation to the variable lap and calculate the equation of the least squares regression line for the transformed data. Round the values of the intercept and slope to one decimal place. (2 marks)
time = 30.3 + 32.3 × log₁₀(lap)
bApply a squared transformation to the variable time and calculate the equation of the least squares regression line for the transformed data. Round the values of the intercept and slope to one decimal place. (2 marks)
(time)² = 820.7 + 367.5 × lap
cWhich transformation is the best fit for the data set? (1 mark)
time = 30.3 + 32.3 × log₁₀(lap) (r² = 0.994 vs 0.975 for the squared transformation)
