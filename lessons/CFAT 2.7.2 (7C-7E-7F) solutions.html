Question 73 marks

Steve buys a smartphone for $550. It depreciates by 10% of the sale price every year.

Edrolo U3&4 5D Q4
aWrite a rule to determine Sₙ, the value of the smartphone after n years. (1 mark)
d = 10% × 550 = 55
Sₙ = 550 − 55n
bWhat is the value of the smartphone after the third year? (1 mark)
S₃ = 550 − 55 × 3 = $385
cAfter how many years will the smartphone have no value? (1 mark)
550 − 55n = 0 → n = 10 years
Question 83 marks

Janine put $400 into her savings account when she opened it. It pays simple interest at a rate of 3% per month.

Edrolo U3&4 5E Q11
aConstruct a rule to calculate the balance of the account, Jₙ, at month n. (1 mark)
d = 3% × 400 = 12
Jₙ = 400 + 12n
bHow much money will be in the account after six months? (1 mark)
J₆ = 400 + 12 × 6 = $472
cAfter how many months will the balance exceed $500? (1 mark)
400 + 12n > 500 → n > 8.33 → 9 months
