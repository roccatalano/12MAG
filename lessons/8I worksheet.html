8I Calculating the paymentExample, then you try
Example 30 · Calculating the payment from a perpetuity
You try 1

Elizabeth invests her superannuation payout of $500 000 into a perpetuity that will provide a monthly income. If the interest rate for the perpetuity is 6% per annum, what monthly payment will Elizabeth receive?

D = r ÷ (100 × p) × V0 = ______ ÷ (100 × ______) × ______

D = 6 ÷ (100 × 12) × 500 000 = 2500
Elizabeth will receive $2500 every month.

Elise invests $259 920 in a perpetuity. Her bank offers an interest rate of 4.8% per annum, compounding monthly.

a. What is the monthly amount she will receive from the bank?

d = 4.8 ÷ (12 × 100) × 259 920 = 1039.68
$1039.68 each month
8I Calculating the investment requiredExample, then you try
Example 31 · Calculating the investment required to establish a perpetuity
You try 2

Calculate how much money will need to be invested in a perpetuity account, earning interest of 4.8% per annum compounding monthly, if $300 will be withdrawn every month.

______ = ______ ÷ (100 × ______) × V0

300 = 4.8 ÷ (100 × 12) × V₀ = 0.004 × V₀
V₀ = 300 ÷ 0.004 = 75 000: $75 000 must be invested

Calculate how much money will need to be invested in a perpetuity account, earning interest of 3.6% per annum compounding monthly, if $450 will be withdrawn every month.

450 = 3.6 ÷ (100 × 12) × V₀ = 0.003 × V₀
V₀ = 450 ÷ 0.003 = $150 000
8I Calculating the interest rateExample, then you try
Example 32 · Calculating the interest rate of a perpetuity
You try 3

A university mathematics faculty has $30 000 to invest. It intends to award an annual mathematics prize of $1500 with the interest earned from investing this money in a perpetuity. What is the minimum interest rate that will allow this prize to be awarded indefinitely?

______ = r ÷ (100 × ______) × ______

1500 = r ÷ (100 × 1) × 30 000 = r × 300
r = 1500 ÷ 300 = 5: the minimum rate is 5% per annum

A sports club has $40 000 to invest. It intends to award an annual prize of $1800 with the interest earned from investing this money in a perpetuity. What is the minimum interest rate that will allow this prize to be awarded indefinitely?

1800 = r ÷ 100 × 40 000 = r × 400
r = 1800 ÷ 400 = 4.5: 4.5% per annum
