Key ideas

Key definitions and formulas

Key definitions

Recurrence relationgives the NEXT term from the current one: step by step
Explicit rulegives any term Vn straight from n: no stepping
Simple interest · flat ratelinear: the same amount D (= r% of V0) added / subtracted each year
Unit costlinear decay: D = depreciation per unit of use (per hour, per km)
Compound interest · reducing balancegeometric: × R each year (R > 1 growth, R < 1 decay)
Interest earned · total depreciationVn − V0 · V0 − Vn

Key formulas

Linear ruleVn = V0 ± nDD = r/100 × V0 (simple interest, flat rate)
Geometric ruleVn = Rn × V0R = 1 + r/100 (growth) · R = 1 − r/100 (decay)
Solving for nround n UPinterest / depreciation happens at the END of each period

From recurrence relation to explicit rule (linear)

Skip the steps: jump straight to term n

  • after n steps, D has been added (or subtracted) n times
  • growth: Vn = V0 + nD · decay: Vn = V0 − nD
  • simple interest and flat rate: D = r/100 × V0 · unit cost: D = cost per unit
Linear ruleVn = V0 ± nD
$2000 at 5% simple interest: V0 = 2000,   Vn+1 = Vn + 100
V1 = 2000 + 1 × 100 · V2 = 2000 + 2 × 100 · V3 = 2000 + 3 × 100
Vn = 2000 + 100n  →  V15 = 2000 + 15 × 100 = $3500
Count the steps: the subscript tells you how many times D was added.

Explicit rule for geometric growth and decay

Multiply by R, n times

  • Vn = Rn × V0 (same rule for growth and decay)
  • compound interest: R = 1 + r/100 · reducing balance: R = 1 − r/100
  • interest earned = Vn − V0 · total depreciation = V0 − Vn
Geometric ruleVn = Rn × V0R = 1 ± r/100
$2000 at 5% compound interest: V0 = 2000,   Vn+1 = 1.05Vn
V1 = 1.05 × 2000 · V2 = 1.052 × 2000 · V3 = 1.053 × 2000
Vn = 1.05n × 2000  →  V18 = 1.0518 × 2000 = $4813.24
Interest IN one year = Vn − Vn−1; interest OVER n years = Vn − V0.

Solving a geometric rule for n or r

How many years for $5000 at 6% p.a. compound interest to grow above $8000?
