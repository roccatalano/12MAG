Question 62 marks

The least-squares regression line for a data set is Weight = 3.8 × Age + 12.5, where weight is in kilograms and age is in months. Interpret the gradient and the y-intercept in the context of this model.

The gradient of 3.8 means that for each additional month of age, the weight is predicted to increase by 3.8 kg. The y-intercept of 12.5 means that at birth (age 0 months), the predicted weight is 12.5 kg.

Question 142 marks

A full regression analysis was carried out on the age, in years, and the annual maintenance cost, in dollars, of 12 ride-on mowers at a Geelong council depot. The mowers were aged from 1 to 9 years. The least-squares regression line is cost = 180 + 95 × age, Pearson's correlation coefficient is r = 0.96 and the residual plot shows no clear pattern. Predict the annual maintenance cost of a 6-year-old mower and state whether this prediction is reliable. Give a reason for your answer.

180 + 95 × 6 = $750. The prediction is reliable: it is an interpolation (within 1 to 9 years), the association is strong and linear and the residual plot supports a linear model.
